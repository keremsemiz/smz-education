About SMZ Education

A school website was just the beginning.

I first thought about SMZ Education after seeing a red schoolhouse donation box in an Istanbul bookshop. I had recently learned to build websites and wondered whether I could help a school that way.

That first idea has grown into a Berlin-based non-profit providing websites, email, hosting, and learning tools to schools free of charge.

Our mission

Why we do this.

A school shouldn't need a large technology budget to have a dependable online presence.

When resources are limited, a website, school email system, or student portal can be difficult to prioritise. But these are practical tools: they help families find information, staff communicate, and students access school resources.

SMZ Education creates and maintains those tools for partner schools at no cost. We ask what each school needs, build around those needs, and stay available when something needs changing.

01

School websites

Clear, up-to-date websites where schools can introduce themselves, publish information, and be found by parents and their communities.

02

Email, hosting and support

Reliable everyday infrastructure, with updates and technical help handled by us so school staff can concentrate on their work.

03

E-Classroom

Our adaptable school platform, shaped by the language, features, and workflows that partner schools actually ask for.

Looking ahead

What we want to achieve.

The goal isn't simply to launch more websites. It's to make sure the digital services we provide remain useful, accessible, and sustainable as more schools join.

  • Reach schools that have little or no dependable digital presence.
  • Keep core services free, including the help schools need after launch.
  • Improve E-Classroom in response to feedback from teachers and administrators.

How it happened

Our story, so far.

There wasn't a fixed plan at the start. Each new project came from a conversation with a school and something it needed.

The bookshop donation box.

A small red schoolhouse collecting donations in a bookshop made me wonder what I could contribute with the web-development skills I was learning.

2024Senegal

Our first school: Shiloh Académie.

After searching for a school that could use a website, I contacted Shiloh Académie in Senegal and built its website for free. Its response encouraged me to continue.

NextE-Classroom

Building a portal that actually fit.

Shiloh asked for a student portal. I first set up RosarioSIS, but its language support and limited customisation didn't suit the school. That feedback led me to build our own adaptable system, E-Classroom.

From the founder

A note from Kerem.

I started SMZ Education while I was still at school myself. I liked building websites, but I wanted to use what I'd learned for someone else. The first school website showed me how useful a fairly simple project could be.

The most interesting part of the work is still the conversation beforehand: finding out what a teacher, administrator, or principal actually needs, then figuring out how to make it work. And we don't disappear after a launch. We keep updating and improving the services alongside the schools.

— Kerem
