About Our Work

SMZ Education is a non-profit organization that provides schools with free technology services. We work closely with each school to address their unique needs, develop customized online platforms, and offer ongoing guidance to help them leverage modern educational tools.

SMZ Education initially began when Kerem Semiz, the founder, spotted a donation box for underserved schools in a bookshop in Turkey. He wondered what he could do to help using his own skillset, and thus SMZ Education was born. SMZ Education has been supporting schools since late 2024 and was officially registered as a German Non-Profit Organization in March 2026, following a lengthy process.

Our core mission is to ensure that every school, regardless of their resources, can benefit from technology. SMZ Education creates and hosts websites, online learning platforms, and digital school management tools for underdeveloped schools—completely free of charge. We believe that technology can be a great equalizer in education, and we are dedicated to making this a reality everywhere.

We continuously adapt our offerings based on feedback from schools and advancements in the tech sector. By doing so, we ensure each collaboration remains innovative, sustainable, and impactful. Our approach is centered on empowering educators and students with tools that enhance learning and streamline school operations.

Our direction

Technology that schools can actually use

We are building a dependable service, not a one-off campaign. The tools we provide should remain useful to a school long after launch.

01 / Mission

Give schools the digital basics they need at no cost.

We design, host, and maintain practical websites, email systems, and online services for schools that would otherwise struggle to access them.

02 / Vision

Make a school's location irrelevant to its digital opportunities.

Every school should be able to communicate professionally, share its work, and participate in modern education regardless of geography or budget.

How we work

Four principles guide every project.

They keep the work focused on the school, easy to maintain, and useful in daily life.
