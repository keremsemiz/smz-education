Our mission

Why I do this.

A school shouldn't need a large technology budget to have a dependable online presence.

When resources are limited, a website, school email system, or student portal can be difficult to prioritise. But these are practical tools: they help families find information, staff communicate, and students access school resources.

The internet provides a brilliant opportunity for students to learn new skills at their own pace. SMZ Education simplifies this by creating and maintaining digital tools for partner schools at no cost. We ask what each school needs, build around those needs, and stay available when something needs changing.

01

School websites

Schools need an online presence to connect with their community and students. We create and maintain clean, practical and functional websites for each school so that they can easily be found, and provide a platform for sharing information and resources.

02

Email, hosting and support

In the new digital age, anyone can have a website, but the true scale behind hosting, maintainance, and support is often overlooked. We provide comprehensive email, hosting, and support services that can be adjusted to meet the specific needs of each school.

03

E-Classroom

Our adaptable digital school platform has over 35,000 users across 41 schools. E-Classroom enables students to access learning materials and resources from anywhere, 24/7, enables teachers to manage their classes and students to engage with the curriculum and enables school administrators to keep track of entire school operations and timetables.

Looking ahead

What we want to achieve.

The goal isn't simply to launch more websites. It's to make sure the digital services we provide remain useful, accessible, and sustainable as more schools join.

  • Reach rural schools that have no dependable digital presence.
  • Keep all services including design, hosting and E-Classroom free, forever
  • Improve and add more features to E-Classroom based on user feedback.
  • Expand our reach to more countries, schools and communities.

The digital divide in schools

Internet access at school is still far from universal.

In 2024, fewer than half of primary schools worldwide had internet access for teaching. The difference between primary and secondary schools is substantial, even as access improves.

This is why practical, affordable digital tools matter. SMZ Education doesn't install internet connections: we provide the websites, email, hosting, and learning services that schools can use once connected.

How it happened

Our story, so far.

2024Istanbul, Türkiye

The bookshop donation box.

A small red schoolhouse collecting donations in a bookshop made me wonder what I could contribute with the web-development skills I was learning.

2024Senegal

Our first school: Shiloh Académie.

After searching for a school that could use a website, I found Shiloh Académie in Senegal who was looking for help, and built its website for free. Its response encouraged me to continue.

2025E-Classroom

Building a portal that actually fit.

Shiloh asked for a student portal. I first set up RosarioSIS, but its language support and limited customisation didn't suit the school. That feedback led me to build our own adaptable system, E-Classroom.

From the founder and sole member

A note from Kerem.

I started SMZ Education when I was 15 years old, and my first school website showed me how useful a fairly simple project could be.

The most interesting part of my work is the ongoing collaboration with the schools. I get to understand their unique challenges and work together to find solutions that truly make a difference in their daily operations.

As a current high-school student myself, I know the challenges students face and how accessible technology can help overcome them. I've been working with schools ever since we launched, and it is a truly rewarding experience that I can use my skills to make a real impact.

SMZ Education is still a one-person operation. I speak directly with school staff, design and develop their websites, build E-Classroom, maintain the services, and answer support requests myself. Being involved at every stage means I know each school's needs firsthand, and I'm still there when something needs changing months after launch.

- Kerem
