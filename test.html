Our History

Building Dreams Since Inception

Great Stars Montessori School was established with a clear vision: to provide quality education that is accessible to families in the Dansoman community and beyond. Our founders recognized the need for a school that combines academic excellence with moral values and character development.

Located in the heart of Dansoman, one of Accra's most vibrant residential districts known for its educational institutions, we have grown to become a trusted name in private education, serving families who seek the best for their children.

Today, we continue to uphold our founding principles while adapting to modern educational practices, ensuring our students are prepared for the challenges and opportunities of tomorrow.

FAQ

Common Questions

What are your school hours?

Our school is open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Classes typically run from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, with after-school care available until 5:00 PM.

What age can my child start?

Children can join our nursery program from age 2. We accept students at all levels throughout the academic year, subject to availability.

How do I get fee information?

Please visit our school office or call us at +233 24 225 0493 for detailed fee information. Our fees are structured to be affordable while maintaining quality education standards.

Is there a school uniform?

Yes, all students are required to wear the official school uniform. Uniforms can be purchased from the school. Details are provided upon admission.

Do you offer transportation?

Please contact our office for current transportation options and routes serving the Dansoman area and surrounding communities.
